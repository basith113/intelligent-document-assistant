# Alder Studio HR Policy

This is a fictional policy for testing the Knowledge AI Assistant.

## Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. They may carry over up to five unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on March 31.

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave each calendar year. Any sick leave absence longer than three consecutive workdays requires a medical certificate.

## Working arrangements

Employees may work remotely up to three days each week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. in the employee's local time.

## Learning allowance

Full-time employees may request reimbursement for up to $750 in professional development expenses per calendar year. Manager approval is required before purchase.
